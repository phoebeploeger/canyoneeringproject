About Canyoneering

Canyonerring is an activite that you and your family or you and your friends can do during the summer time. You can get a tour guide when you are strating out with canyoneering. The tour guide will have the equipment that you guys are using for canyoneering, and will show you how to use the equipment properly. The first part is repelling. Repelling is were you are loewring yourself down on rope with use a tool called reppel device and a climbing harness. The second step is swimming. Swimming is were a canyon has a park of water that you have to swim through to get to the other side. Depending on how deep the water is you could walk through the water or swim through the water. The tour guide mit say to get a wetsuit if the water is pretty high, because the water is very cooled. The last part is hiking. Depending on where you will come out of the canyon you mit to climb over rocks or through drit. Hiking is were you come out of the canyon and you have to hike out to your car.
